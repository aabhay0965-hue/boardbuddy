Introduction

The lifelines of the national economy refer to the transport and communication systems that help in the movement of goods, people, and information across the country and the world.

Importance of Lifelines

  • Helps in economic development of the country.
  • Connects rural and urban areas.
  • Supports trade and commerce.
  • Promotes national integration.
  • Improves accessibility to markets.

Types of Transport

1. Roadways

  • Most widely used transport in India.
  • Connects remote areas.
  • Flexible and cheaper for short distances.

2. Railways

  • Largest railway network in Asia.
  • Important for long-distance travel and freight.
  • Economical for heavy goods transport.

Road Network in India (Basic Map Idea)

📌 IMPORTANT HIGHWAYS:

  • Golden Quadrilateral → Delhi–Mumbai–Chennai–Kolkata
  • North-South Corridor → Srinagar to Kanyakumari
  • East-West Corridor → Silchar to Porbandar

🧠 Map questions come directly from these routes.

Railways Importance

  • Connects major cities and industrial centres.
  • Supports economic integration.
  • Important for passenger and freight transport.

NCERT Insight

Transport systems act as the backbone of a country’s economy by connecting production centers with markets and consumers.

Airways

Air transport is the fastest mode of transport. It is used for long-distance travel, urgent cargo, and connecting remote areas like mountains and islands.

Features

  • Fastest mode of transport
  • Expensive compared to other modes
  • Useful for difficult terrains (mountains, deserts, islands)

Major Airlines in India

  • Air India
  • IndiGo
  • SpiceJet
  • Vistara

Waterways

Water transport is the cheapest mode of transport for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances.

Types of Waterways

1. Inland Waterways

  • Rivers and canals used for transport.
  • Examples: Ganga, Brahmaputra

2. Sea Routes

  • Used for international trade.
  • Major ports handle import-export activities.

Major Ports of India

  • Mumbai Port
  • Chennai Port
  • Kolkata Port
  • Kandla Port
  • Visakhapatnam Port

Pipelines

Pipelines are used for transporting liquids and gases like petroleum, natural gas, and water over long distances.

Advantages

  • Continuous supply of materials
  • Safe and efficient
  • Reduces transport cost

Major Pipeline Networks

  • From oil fields in Assam to refineries in Bihar
  • From Gujarat to northern India for petroleum products

Communication System

Communication connects people and businesses by exchanging information across distances.

Types of Communication

  • Personal Communication: Letters, mobile phones, emails
  • Mass Communication: Television, newspapers, radio, internet

Importance of Communication

  • Spreads information quickly
  • Supports business and trade
  • Helps in education and awareness
  • Connects rural and urban areas

NCERT Insight

Modern transport and communication systems have reduced distances between people and are essential for global economic integration.

International Trade

International trade is the exchange of goods and services between countries. It plays a key role in the economic development of a nation.

Importance of International Trade

  • Earns foreign exchange for the country
  • Encourages specialization in production
  • Increases economic growth
  • Improves global relations

Imports and Exports

Exports

  • Goods and services sold to other countries
  • Examples: Tea, cotton textiles, engineering goods, software services

Imports

  • Goods and services purchased from other countries
  • Examples: Petroleum, machinery, chemicals, electronic goods

Balance of Trade

Balance of trade refers to the difference between the value of exports and imports of a country.

  • Favourable Trade: When exports are greater than imports
  • Unfavourable Trade: When imports are greater than exports

Tourism as a Trade Support System

Tourism is an important industry that supports international trade by generating foreign exchange and employment.

Importance

  • Boosts national income
  • Creates employment opportunities
  • Promotes cultural exchange
  • Supports transport and hotel industries

Transport and Globalisation

Modern transport and communication systems have connected the world, leading to globalisation.

  • Faster movement of goods and services
  • Easy international communication
  • Growth of multinational companies

India in Global Trade

India participates actively in global trade by exporting goods and services and importing essential resources.

Main Export Items

  • Textiles
  • Software services
  • Engineering goods
  • Agricultural products

Main Import Items

  • Petroleum
  • Machinery
  • Electronics
  • Chemicals

NCERT Insight

International trade acts as a bridge between countries and strengthens economic relationships in the globalized world.
